# 100 most asked questions for senior product designers and design engineers

Questions hiring managers, design directors, and staff engineers actually ask at senior level. The first 60 apply to both roles. The last 40 split by craft: product design judgment vs. design-engineering execution.

These are screening, deep-dive, and panel questions — not take-home prompts.

---

## 1. Craft and process (1–15)

1. Walk me through a product you shipped end to end. What was the problem, the constraint, and the outcome?
2. How do you decide what to design next when everything feels important?
3. How do you go from a vague problem to a testable hypothesis?
4. When do you skip research, and when is that a mistake?
5. How do you choose between qualitative research, analytics, and expert judgment?
6. Show me a time you killed your own idea. What changed your mind?
7. How do you run a design critique that actually improves the work?
8. What’s your bar for “good enough to ship” vs. “needs another pass”?
9. How do you document decisions so a team can move without you?
10. How do you handle conflicting feedback from PM, eng, and leadership?
11. What’s your process when a stakeholder wants a feature that the data doesn’t support?
12. How do you design for edge cases without bloating the happy path?
13. How do you know a design is ready for engineering handoff?
14. What’s the last thing you changed in a live product because of post-launch learning?
15. How do you keep craft high when the sprint is already committed?

---

## 2. Systems, craft, and consistency (16–30)

16. How do you decide what belongs in a design system vs. what stays local to a product?
17. Walk me through how you would start or reboot a design system on a product that already shipped.
18. How do you prevent a design system from becoming a bottleneck?
19. What’s the difference between a component, a pattern, and a template — and when do you use each?
20. How do you handle one-off UI that doesn’t fit the system?
21. How do you version, deprecate, and migrate design-system components?
22. How do you keep Figma and production in sync?
23. What’s your approach to tokens — color, type, space, motion — across platforms?
24. How do you design accessibility in, rather than audit it in at the end?
25. What’s your WCAG bar, and how do you enforce it with engineers?
26. How do you design for dark mode, theming, or white-label without exploding variants?
27. How do you review visual quality at senior level without becoming the “pixel police”?
28. Show me a UI you consider well-crafted. What specifically makes it good?
29. How do you raise the quality of other designers’ work without taking over the file?
30. What’s a system decision you regret, and what would you do now?

---

## 3. Product sense and strategy (31–45)

31. How do you tell a product problem from a design problem?
32. What’s a product you use that is poorly designed, and how would you fix it without a rewrite?
33. How do you define success for a design before it ships?
34. How do you work with PMs on roadmap, sequencing, and scope?
35. When should a senior designer push back on the brief itself?
36. How do you design for activation, retention, and expansion — not just first-use delight?
37. How do you prioritize UX debt against new features?
38. How do you design for multiple personas without making a product that serves none of them well?
39. How do you approach 0-to-1 vs. iterating on a mature product?
40. What’s your take on AI in the product — where it helps users, and where it hurts trust?
41. How do you design empty states, errors, and recovery so they convert instead of confuse?
42. How do you make tradeoffs between speed, quality, and risk?
43. Tell me about a time the business goal and the user goal conflicted. What did you do?
44. How do you use metrics without designing to the metric?
45. What would you change about our product in the first 90 days, and why?

---

## 4. Collaboration, leadership, and influence (46–60)

46. How do you partner with engineers so design intent survives implementation?
47. How do you work with a PM who wants to jump to UI immediately?
48. How do you give feedback to a mid-level designer who is stuck on polish and missing the problem?
49. How do you mentor without becoming a bottleneck for every decision?
50. How do you influence without authority — especially with senior engineers or founders?
51. Tell me about a conflict on a team and how you resolved it.
52. How do you run a design review with executives who have strong opinions and little context?
53. How do you say no to a founder or VP without damaging trust?
54. What’s your model for design team shape — specialists, generalists, embedded, centralized?
55. How do you hire a product designer or design engineer? What do you look for in a portfolio?
56. How do you onboard someone onto a messy product and a messy system?
57. How do you protect focus time and still be available to the team?
58. Tell me about a time you were wrong in public. What happened next?
59. How do you handle a designer or engineer who consistently ships below the bar?
60. What does “senior” mean to you, vs. staff or lead?

---

## 5. Senior product designer — depth (61–80)

61. Walk me through a case study as if I were a skeptical PM. I will interrupt.
62. How do you structure a research plan when you have one week, not six?
63. How do you synthesize research into something a team will actually use?
64. How do you prototype — paper, Figma, code, Wizard of Oz — and when do you switch?
65. How do you test a concept that users don’t have language for yet?
66. How do you design complex workflows (settings, admin, permissions, multi-step jobs) without drowning the user?
67. How do you approach information architecture for a product that’s grown organically?
68. How do you write UX copy that carries the product instead of decorating it?
69. How do you design onboarding that teaches the product instead of touring it?
70. How do you handle localization, RTL, and content that will be twice as long in another language?
71. Show me a time you used data to change a design after launch.
72. How do you present work so leadership can decide, not just admire?
73. What’s your facilitation toolkit — workshops, critiques, alignment sessions — and when is a workshop a waste?
74. How do you design for trust, consent, and privacy in a product that collects data?
75. How do you approach competitive analysis without copying the category leader?
76. A PM wants a dashboard. How do you find out whether they actually need a dashboard?
77. How do you design for power users and first-time users in the same surface?
78. What’s a portfolio piece you’re least proud of, and what would you redo?
79. How do you keep your taste sharp when most of the week is meetings?
80. If we hired you, what would you need from us to do your best work?

---

## 6. Design engineer — depth (81–100)

81. Walk me through a UI you implemented. What was hard about making it feel like the design?
82. How do you decide what to build in the design system vs. in the product?
83. How do you translate a Figma file into production without losing spacing, type, and motion?
84. What’s your approach to component APIs — props, composition, slots, variants?
85. How do you keep components accessible: keyboard, focus, semantics, announcements?
86. How do you handle responsive layouts that Figma never fully specified?
87. How do you implement motion so it feels intentional, not decorative — and how do you respect reduced motion?
88. What’s your testing strategy for UI: visual regression, interaction tests, a11y checks, snapshot tests?
89. How do you debug a layout that looks right in Figma and wrong in the browser?
90. How do you work with product designers so they design things that are feasible and still ambitious?
91. Tell me about a time you pushed back on a design because of performance, a11y, or maintainability.
92. How do you approach CSS architecture today — Tailwind, CSS modules, vanilla-extract, Panda, something else — and why?
93. How do you ship a breaking change in a component library used by multiple teams?
94. How do you measure and improve UI performance (CLS, INP, bundle size, hydration, animation jank)?
95. How do you prototype in code vs. Figma when exploring a new interaction?
96. What’s your bar for “pixel perfect,” and when is it the wrong bar?
97. How do you handle design QA — who owns the last 10% before launch?
98. Show me a PR or component you’re proud of. What tradeoffs did you make?
99. How do you stay current with frontend and design-system practice without chasing every trend?
100. If you joined as our first design engineer, what would you build in the first 30 / 60 / 90 days?

---

## How to use this list

**For interview prep.** Pick 10 questions in the shared set, 10 in your craft track, and answer each out loud with one story: context, constraint, decision, result. Senior answers name the tradeoff, not just the process.

**For running interviews.** Use 1–15 to test process, 16–30 for systems, 31–45 for product sense, 46–60 for leadership. Then spend the rest of the loop on 61–80 or 81–100 depending on the role. One deep case-study walkthrough beats ten hypotheticals.

**For portfolio and site content.** The questions that keep coming up — shipped outcomes, systems vs. one-offs, AI tradeoffs, 90-day plan — are the ones a case study should already answer on the page, before anyone asks.
