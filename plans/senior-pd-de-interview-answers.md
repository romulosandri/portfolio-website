## First 90 days (method only)

Not a claim about any employer.

1. **Days 1–30:** Use the product as a new user. Sit with support/sales if they exist. Name the job that pays the bills.
2. **Days 31–60:** One leaking user path and one shared-pattern bet (example of the pattern: Meltwater-style universal filters, not a visual refresh).
3. **Days 61–90:** Ship that path, instrument it, write down what will not be done.

**First design engineer variant:** 30 — inventory production vs. Figma, tokenise color/type/space, wrap the top components with accessible APIs and reduced-motion. 60 — one product path on the new language, contribution path so he is not a ticket queue. 90 — versioned library, one documented pattern, optional agent/MCP consumption scoped to *that* system.
