## Why do you want to join us?

I've been following Zeh Fernandes on LinkedIn and X for years. He has genuinely inspired my work since his early tests with Spline, when he created the cube for Resend. I am equally inspired by Zeno's entrepreneurial story as a Brazilian who took over the world.

I've used Resend products (Resend and React Email) in different projects. Most recently, at Pacelane, my last startup, we ran all product email through Resend and React Email. My portfolio contact form runs on it too. So I know first-hand how easy Resend is to implement, and how considered and intentional the team has been with the products.

Resend is also one of the most forward-thinking companies of this AI era. Software is becoming less about interfaces and more about how agents interact with products, and few companies have benefited from that shift as much as Resend. The idea that the documentation is the product is very smart. Most implementation now happens inside an agent, so docs that an agent can read and act on are what make a product accessible to everyone, with zero friction.

Because the product is good (I've used it), and because the team inspires me, I want to join Resend as a Product Designer.

## Tell us about your favorite product from a UX and Design perspective.

"Taste" and "craft" are overused words right now, but it is impossible to describe Granola without them. A very intentional use of micro-animations, for example when the input field (at the bottom, when creating a new note) morphs into a different shape as the live transcript button is clicked. The level of detail and care on every hover interaction, even for small components is unmatched.

Granola has a beautiful blend of invisible software from the new AI era (an agentic layer running in the background) with a very intuitive user interface. The UX makes you almost forget it is right there. A good example of this balance is the briefing feature. It is a subtle detail that emerges right before the meeting starts, with information about the person you are going to talk to. You never asked for it, but it is right there when you need it.

I could go on for hours talking about Granola, the intentional use of mixed media as a way to create this organic feel for the brand, and the simple but effective UX. For Granola, just like Resend, no detail is too small.
